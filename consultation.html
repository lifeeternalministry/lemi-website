What to expect

A conversation first, then a written recommendation.

We want to understand your ministry before we prescribe anything. A discovery consultation gives us time to learn your history, doctrine, current governance, and goals.

Consultation is not affiliation. Receiving services from us does not by itself create covering, credentialing, or membership. Those relationships are entered into deliberately and documented separately.

How an engagement begins

Eight steps, in order.

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Tell us who you are and what you need.

  2. 2

    Discovery consultation

    We listen first to your history, doctrine, governance, and goals.

  3. 3

    Ministry assessment

    Where you are, what is unclear, and what needs strengthening.

  4. 4

    Scope of service

    We agree on what we will and will not do together.

  5. 5

    Written recommendation

    A written proposal, before any commitment.

  6. 6

    Engagement

    An agreement that states scope and boundaries.

  7. 7

    Development and implementation

    Training, structures, and documents that fit your season.

  8. 8

    Follow-up

    Review and continued support as you grow.

Questions

Before you reach out.

Do we have to be affiliated with LEMI?

No. A ministry does not have to be affiliated with Life Eternal Ministry International to receive governance consultation, training, or development services.

Does consultation put us under your covering?

No. Consultation does not automatically create ecclesiastical covering, credentialing, membership, or spiritual oversight. If a ministry or leader chooses that kind of relationship, it is established deliberately and documented in writing.

Will you quote a price right away?

No. We begin with a discovery conversation and an assessment so we understand your ministry first. A written recommendation and scope follow before any commitment.

Do you replace legal, tax, or counseling professionals?

No. Our services do not replace licensed legal, tax, accounting, or mental-health professionals where those services are required. We will tell you when to involve them.

Ministry & Leadership Governance Services

Healthy structure for healthy ministry.

We help churches and ministries build governance that is biblical, clear, and accountable, and that fits their size, season, and tradition.

What governance consultation is

Who leads, how decisions are made, and how everyone stays accountable.

Ecclesiastical governance consultation helps a ministry define its leadership roles, decision-making, recognition of leaders, and accountability. We work from Scripture first, then help you put it in writing.

A ministry does not have to be affiliated with LEMI to receive these services. We serve ministries of any tradition.

Our standard

Office never supersedes character, and accountability accompanies authority.

How an engagement begins

We understand your ministry before we recommend anything.

There are no packages or prices up front. The process starts with a conversation.

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Tell us who you are and what you need.

  2. 2

    Discovery consultation

    We listen first: history, doctrine, governance, leadership, and goals.

  3. 3

    Ministry assessment

    Where you are, what is unclear, and what must be strengthened.

  4. 4

    Scope of service

    We agree on what we will and will not do together.

  5. 5

    Written recommendation

    A written proposal, before any commitment.

  6. 6

    Engagement

    An agreement that states scope and boundaries.

  7. 7

    Development and implementation

    Structures and documents built around your size and season.

  8. 8

    Follow-up

    Review and continued support as the ministry grows.

Clear expectations

Consultation is not affiliation.

What consultation includes

  • Ecclesiastical governance consultation
  • Ministry development and leadership formation
  • Education and organizational development
  • Written recommendations and documents for your ministry

What it does not create

  • Covering, credentialing, membership, or spiritual oversight
  • Authority over your ministry
  • A replacement for licensed legal, tax, accounting, or mental-health professionals

If a ministry or leader chooses a covering or oversight relationship with LEMI, it is entered deliberately and documented in writing, including its authority, boundaries, responsibilities, and method of withdrawal.

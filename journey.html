Leadership & Ministry Development

Heal. Build. Release.

Our leadership journey develops the person before the office. It is the path behind every mentorship, training, and credentialing relationship at LEMI.

Created Before Called

We develop the person before the office.

Before God called you to an office, He created you, knew you, and named you His own. Identity comes before assignment.

That is why LEMI begins with the person: your walk with God, your character, your health, and your home. The calling is built on that foundation, not in place of it.

Genesis 1:27Jeremiah 1:5Ephesians 4:1
  1. The personIdentity in Christ, relationship with God, and wholeness.
  2. The characterHumility, integrity, and a worthy walk.
  3. The homeMarriage and family as the first place of ministry.
  4. The officeRecognized and released on that foundation.
The three phases

One path, walked at your pace.

Phase 1

Heal

Wholeness before function.

Leaders find healing, restoration, and accountability in their walk with God, their minds, their marriages, and their homes.

Phase 2

Build

Formation before recognition.

Character, sound doctrine, and leadership skill are formed so the leader is ready for the assignment.

Phase 3

Release

Function before platform.

Leaders are affirmed, commissioned, and supported as they serve, equip others, and multiply.

Our standard · Ephesians 4:1
Office never supersedes character.

Titles can be given in a day; character is formed over a lifetime. We read the Fivefold offices in the light of the call to walk worthy, so we always ask who a leader is before we ask what a leader can do.

Who the journey is for

Leaders at every stage.

Emerging leaders, established pastors, Fivefold ministers, leadership teams, and leaders returning to service after a season of restoration.

  • Emerging and established leaders
  • Fivefold ministers seeking formation and affirmation
  • Pastors, elders, and ministry teams
  • Leaders in a season of healing or restoration
  • Leaders preparing to plant or launch a work

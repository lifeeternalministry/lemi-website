About us

Equipping leaders to serve biblically, spiritually, and healthily.

Life Eternal Ministry International Inc. is a Spirit-led, Fivefold Ministry organization founded by Apostle Ronique P. Young and Prophetess Monique K. Young, commissioned to train, affirm, and support ministry leaders at every stage of their journey.

Our mission

Healthy leaders build healthy churches, families, and communities.

We equip leaders through biblical alignment, spiritual maturity, and legal integrity. Our goal is to ensure that leaders are not only prepared for ministry but are whole, covered, and aligned with their divine assignment.

Whether you are launching a new work, in need of restructuring, or seeking accountability and growth, Life Eternal Ministry International is here to walk with you.

Our story

Founded on divine revelation and shaped by lived experience.

This journey began during an intimate time of prayer and reflection. That revelation became the seed of a movement focused on wholeness, accountability, and the true heart of leadership.

In 2017, God confirmed this calling with a foundational scripture: John 17:3, “And this is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom You have sent.” (NKJV)

That verse became the cornerstone of our mission: to lead others into an authentic, transforming relationship with the Father through the Son, and to build Kingdom leaders who are biblically grounded, spiritually mature, and emotionally whole.

Meet our founders

Created Before Called

We develop the person before the office.

Before God called you to an office, He created you, knew you, and named you His own. Identity comes before assignment.

That is why LEMI begins with the person: your walk with God, your character, your health, and your home. The calling is built on that foundation, not in place of it.

Genesis 1:27Jeremiah 1:5Ephesians 4:1
  1. The personIdentity in Christ, relationship with God, and wholeness.
  2. The characterHumility, integrity, and a worthy walk.
  3. The homeMarriage and family as the first place of ministry.
  4. The officeRecognized and released on that foundation.
Our biblical mandate

Three passages carry everything we do.

Rooted in
John 17:3

The foundation begins with knowing the only true God and Jesus Christ whom He has sent. Before leadership development, recognition, governance, titles, or assignments, there must be relationship with God through Jesus Christ.

Mandated by
Ephesians 4:11–16

We read the Fivefold offices in the light of verse 1: walk worthy of your calling. Office never supersedes character, and leaders exist to equip the saints until the whole Body matures.

Guided by
Matthew 28:19–20

Healthy governance serves the mission Christ gave His Church: to make disciples, teach, serve, develop others, and take part faithfully in His work.

Who we serve

Individuals and ministries called to serve the Body.

We recognize three connected areas of ministry. Part of our work is helping leaders see the difference between spiritual gifts, ministry functions, offices, positions, and titles.

Fivefold Offices

The equipping gifts of Ephesians 4. We help leaders examine calling, character, formation, function, recognition, authority, and accountability.

  • Apostles
  • Prophets
  • Evangelists
  • Pastors
  • Teachers

Supporting Offices

Necessary roles in the government, care, and service of the Church. Terms and structures vary among traditions, so we work from Scripture first.

  • Bishops and overseers
  • Elders and deacons
  • Other recognized serving offices

Ministry of Helps

The gifts, skills, and faithfulness that keep ministry healthy, whether or not a title is attached.

  • Intercessors
  • Administrators
  • Worship leaders
  • Hospitality, media, finance, outreach, caregiving, and more

Everyone has a place to serve; everyone does not require a title to serve.

How leaders grow

From knowing Him to multiplying leaders.

Nine steps sit inside our three-phase journey: Heal, Build, Release. This is the path behind every consultation, curriculum, and credentialing process.

  1. 1

    Know Him

    Relationship with God comes before responsibility.

  2. 2

    Walk Worthy

    Humility, gentleness, patience, and love. Office never outranks character.

  3. 3

    Be Formed

    Formation comes before recognition.

  4. 4

    Understand the Calling

    Gift, function, office, and title are different things.

  5. 5

    Be Equipped

    Teaching, mentoring, and accountability for the assignment.

  6. 6

    Function

    Serve faithfully in the place you have been given.

  7. 7

    Equip Others

    Fivefold leaders prepare the saints for the work of ministry.

  8. 8

    Mature

    A stable, loving, functioning Body where every part does its share.

  9. 9

    Multiply

    Leaders who raise leaders, and ministries that plant ministries.

Our standard · Ephesians 4:1
Office never supersedes character.

Titles can be given in a day; character is formed over a lifetime. We read the Fivefold offices in the light of the call to walk worthy, so we always ask who a leader is before we ask what a leader can do.

How we lead

Healthy Leaders Lead & Serve Healthy.

Five principles shape every conversation, assessment, and recommendation.

  • Character before title.Who the leader is matters alongside what the leader can do.
  • Formation before recognition.Recognition should confirm development, never replace it.
  • Function before platform.Ministry office exists for service and equipping, not visibility.
  • Accountability accompanies authority.More responsibility never means less accountability.
  • Love governs leadership.First Corinthians 13 applies in correction, conflict, and decisions.
Clear expectations

What we are, and what we are not.

What we provide

  • Ecclesiastical governance consultation
  • Ministry development and leadership formation
  • Education and organizational development
  • Services for ministries of any tradition. A ministry does not have to be affiliated with LEMI to receive them.

What we do not do

  • Replace licensed legal, tax, accounting, or mental-health professionals where those services are required
  • Create covering, credentialing, membership, or spiritual oversight automatically
  • Assume authority over your ministry through consultation alone

Receiving governance consultation from LEMI does not by itself create ecclesiastical covering, credentialing, denominational affiliation, membership, or spiritual oversight. Any such relationship is entered into deliberately and documented separately.

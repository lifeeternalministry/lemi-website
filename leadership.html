Leadership team

Empowering leaders through biblical training, mentorship, and accountability.

Life Eternal Ministry International was founded by Apostle Ronique P. Young and Prophetess Monique K. Young, who co-labor to raise biblically sound, spiritually mature, and legally structured leaders.

Apostle · Founder

Apostle Ronique P. Young

Founder | Network Engineer | Mentor | Visionary | Husband & Father

Apostle Ronique P. Young is a visionary leader, teacher, and technologist whose life is devoted to building spiritually strong individuals, families, and ministries. He is the Founder of Life Eternal Ministry International Inc., a Fivefold Ministry organization dedicated to equipping leaders to serve biblically, spiritually, and healthily.

In 2024 he graduated from Colorado Technical University with a bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, specializing in Network Management, further equipping him to merge faith with innovation. He is also the visionary of Young Tech, an initiative focused on empowering the next generation through technology and leadership development.

His journey of healing, accountability, and understanding his identity as a son of God has shaped him into a stronger husband, father, and Kingdom leader. He believes that healthy men produce healthy homes, and healthy homes build healthy churches and communities.

When a man is healed and whole, he becomes a pillar for his family, his ministry, and his community.
Prophetess · Co-Founder

Prophetess Monique K. Young

Visionary | Coach | Mentor | Speaker | Author | Wife & Mother

Prophetess Monique K. Young is a Spirit-led, biblically grounded leader committed to equipping Kingdom ambassadors through prayer, sound doctrine, prophetic insight, and strategic leadership training. She is the Co-Founder of Life Eternal Ministry International Inc., where she and her husband, Apostle Ronique P. Young, co-labor to raise biblically sound, spiritually mature, and legally structured leaders for Kingdom impact.

She is passionate about helping individuals discover their identity in Christ, walk in their God-given purpose, and lead with integrity. A certified John Maxwell Leadership Coach, speaker, and author, she has developed tools and teachings that build up individuals, marriages, and ministries. Her written works include 21 Days in His Presence, the Covenant Keepers series, and curriculum for Fivefold Ministry Training.

Her vision is rooted in Ephesians 4:11–16: to see leaders restored, equipped, and walking in full maturity to serve biblically, spiritually, and healthily.

I’m not just called to minister to the nations; I’m called to model it in my home first.
Leadership & accountability

We ask of ourselves what we ask of every leader.

Accountability is not something LEMI offers others while standing outside it.

  • Accountability accompanies authority.More responsibility never means less accountability, for the leaders we serve or for us.
  • Office never supersedes character.Our titles do not exempt us from the standard of Ephesians 4.
  • Ministry begins at home.Our marriage and family are the first place we model what we teach.
  • Clear and in writing.Any covering, credentialing, or oversight relationship is entered deliberately, with its authority, boundaries, and method of withdrawal documented.
  • Wise counsel.We involve licensed legal, financial, and mental-health professionals wherever they are needed.

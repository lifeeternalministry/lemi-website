Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last reviewed October 27, 2026

Life Eternal Ministry International Inc. wants everyone to be able to learn about our ministry, read our teaching, and reach us, including people who use assistive technology.

Our goal

We work to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. These guidelines describe how to make web content usable for people with disabilities, including people who are blind or have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or have motor or cognitive disabilities.

What we have done

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, and a “Skip to content” link appears on the first press of the Tab key.
  • Pages use clear headings and landmarks so screen readers can move through them easily.
  • Text and buttons are checked for color contrast against their backgrounds.
  • Images that carry meaning have text descriptions; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Every form field has a visible label, and form messages are announced to screen readers.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the site turns off its moving background effects.
  • Pages are tested with automated accessibility checks before each update.

Known limitations

Automated checks do not catch everything, and some older blog content was moved over from a previous website. If any part of the site is hard for you to use, we want to know so we can fix it.

Tell us about a problem

Email us at info.lifeeternalministry@gmail.com or use our contact form. Please tell us the page and what happened. We aim to respond within five business days and, if you need it, to provide the information in another format.
